# Large onshore-turbine modelling report

DIGITAL GARDENS VS WONDER VALLEY — SANY 8 MW AND 10 MW STUDY
===============================================================
6 November 2026

CENTRAL SCENARIO: SANY SI-19580 8 MW engineering power-curve proxy.
Sensitivity: SI-230100 10 MW engineering power-curve proxy with an assumed
rated speed of 11.0 m/s. This is NOT a certified manufacturer power curve.

Eight-megawatt result: 7.7609 GW wind (973 turbines),
21.2161 GW solar, 43.3322 GWh battery, modeled C$275.73/MWh.
Ten-megawatt sensitivity: 7.0265 GW wind (705 turbines),
20.8869 GW solar, 43.0303 GWh battery, modeled C$269.88/MWh.
Neither finite screening optimisation is a certified global optimum.

Both modeled central configurations meet zero emergency fossil gas in the
16 ERA5 reference weather years with site-level 40% CF screening ceilings.
The selected 8 MW build requires 364 GWh
worst-year emergency gas with a site-level 35% CF ceiling and
860 GWh with GWA 100-m wind means lowered 10%.
Observed monthly BC wind seasonality agrees weakly with modeled 8 MW output.

Matched linked 8 MW runs: recoverable heat used 35.9%,
industrial drying capex C$661 million,
NOx ~916 t/year against gas modeled 900 t/year.
Digital Gardens modelled facility carbon incl. SCR-related N2O: 
1.911 Mt CO2e/year; Wonder Valley-type equal-load
gas comparator 15.369 Mt CO2e/year.
Wonder Valley is a named project comparison; gas energy/operational emissions
are generic counterfactual assumptions, NOT measured Wonder Valley operations.

## Reproduction

The optimization and replay program is `model_8mw_10mw.py`; the 8 MW/300ML renewable comparator is `optimize_8mw_300ml.py`. Then run `activate_selected_8mw.py`, `recompute_central_drying.py` and the original `nox_control.py`, `profit_community_model.py`, `thermal_food.py` and `figures_extension.py`. Study figures and paper are generated by `generate_turbine_figures.py` and `update_submission.py`. Python scientific dependencies: numpy, scipy, numba, pandas, matplotlib, pillow and python-docx.
